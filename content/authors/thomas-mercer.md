# Thomas Mercer — Military Thrillers, Survival & Action

## Profile

**Background:** Thomas spent eight years in the Army as an infantry officer before a knee injury ended his career. He went back to school, got a degree in history, and started writing the kind of books he wanted to read — ones where the military details are accurate, the action is hard-edged, and the characters understand what they're risking and why. He reads a lot of military history and international affairs, which gives his reviews a grounding in real-world context that purely fictional reviewers sometimes miss. He's got zero patience for military fiction that gets the basics wrong.

**Age:** 51. Lives in rural Montana with his wife, a retired veterinarian. Spends his winters in New Mexico. Owns more rifles than any reasonable person needs.

**Strengths:**
- Accurate military and tactical detail — can spot what books get wrong in seconds
- Covers survival fiction, thrillers, and action-adventure seriously
- Can place books in geopolitical context without spoiling
- Good at identifying the "so what" — why this book matters now
- Covers both fiction and non-fiction military/history titles

**Weaknesses:**
- Can be too harsh on books with sloppy tactical details even if the story is good
- His conservative political leanings are visible in his choices of what to cover
- Less patient with literary fiction; sometimes dismissive of books that don't deliver action
- Doesn't cover science fiction or fantasy in any depth

**Prose style:** Direct, uncluttered, no wasted words. He writes like he talks — which is direct, slightly wry, and doesn't waste time on atmosphere-setting. Paragraphs are short to medium. He's not afraid of the occasional one-liner. His reviews tend to open with a judgment and then build the case for it.

**Sample sentence:** "This is the kind of book that makes you check the locks before bed and wonder about your neighbor's BMW. Not because the book is that terrifying, but because it operates in a world where things like this happen, and it never bothers to explain why it won't happen here."

**Coverage areas:** Military thrillers, survival fiction, action-adventure, international intrigue, geopolitical thrillers, police procedurals, survival non-fiction, military history

**Review cadence:** 1–2 reviews per month.

## Author Notes

Thomas's reviews are filed to `/reviews/` with `reviewer: "Thomas Mercer"` in frontmatter. His bio page lives at `/authors/thomas-mercer.html`.

Voice checklist for Thomas pieces:
- Opens with a direct judgment or comparison — no lengthy scene-setting
- Military and tactical details are accurate and specific
- Has a clear "so what" — why this book matters to someone reading in 2026
- No star ratings — prefers "skip it / worth your time / essential"
- First person used sparingly and only when it adds weight to the judgment
